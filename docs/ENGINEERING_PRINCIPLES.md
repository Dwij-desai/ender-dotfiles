# EnderOS Engineering Principles

These laws govern engineering decisions across EnderOS.

## Law 1

Never duplicate responsibility.

## Law 2

Portability is more valuable than novelty.

## Law 3

Every dependency must justify its existence.

## Law 4

Every visual component must serve a function.

## Law 5

Everything should gracefully degrade.

## Law 6

No configuration should depend on hidden state.

## Law 7

A fresh Arch install must always be a supported target.
